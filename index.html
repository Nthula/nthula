02 / About Me

Understanding technology. Improving systems. Solving practical problems.

My Information Systems degree at the University of Botswana taught me to see technology as part of a larger picture: the people who use it, the data it holds, and the organisation it serves.

I currently work as an IT Officer. Before that, I completed an industrial attachment in the Bank of Botswana's ICT function, where I helped troubleshoot technical issues, supported device compliance checks, and assisted with telecommunications systems. It made clear how closely day-to-day IT support is tied to the way an organisation runs.

I am interested in systems and business analysis: understanding requirements, examining workflows, and finding where technology could work better for the people using it. I want to build on my technical foundation by developing stronger analytical and requirements-gathering skills.

How I approach technology and business problems
