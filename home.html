About

From coursework to a live support desk.

Between a business problem and a working system, there's usually a database, a process, and someone who has to use it every day — that's the part I'm interested in.

Information Systems sits between business and technology, which is what drew me to it. At the University of Botswana I studied databases, systems analysis and design, business intelligence, and information systems management — a way of looking at problems that starts with people and process, not just code.
